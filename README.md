# Cybersecurity Training Program

A self-paced path from zero to job-ready in cybersecurity. It pairs a **roadmap**
(what to learn, in what order, with free resources) with **hands-on practice**
you do on your own machine.

> **Ground rule:** only test systems you own or have written permission to test.
> Practice in your own VMs or on legal training platforms. Unauthorized access is
> a crime almost everywhere (e.g. the US CFAA, the UK Computer Misuse Act).

## How to use this repo

1. Read the roadmap phase you're on (start at Phase 1 unless you already have IT experience).
2. Work through each phase's checkpoint list and tick items off as you go. Commit your progress; your commit history becomes proof of consistent work.
3. Write up what you learned for each topic in a `notes/` folder. Those write-ups are the start of your portfolio.

## The roadmap

| Phase | Focus | Time | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| [1. Foundations](roadmap/01-foundations.md) | Networking, Linux, Windows, Python | 8–10 wks | OverTheWire Bandit levels 0–20 |
| [2. Security Core](roadmap/02-security-core.md) | CIA, crypto, auth, threats, risk | 6–8 wks | CompTIA Security+ |

After Phase 2, follow the **GRC (Governance, Risk & Compliance) track** in
[career/transferable-skills.md](career/transferable-skills.md). It is tailored to
a background in education compliance and accreditation, and it maps existing
experience to security GRC roles, certifications, portfolio projects and resume
wording.

## Weekly rhythm that works

- **3 sessions of study** (course, reading, videos), about 60% of your time
- **2 sessions of hands-on** (TryHackMe, OverTheWire, CTFs), about 40%
- **1 short write-up** each week: what you did, what broke, what you learned
